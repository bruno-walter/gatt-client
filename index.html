How to use

  1. Click Connect and choose your device (ESP32-C3 Mouse Jiggler or other BLE device).
  2. Once connected, click Discover Services to browse the GATT database.
  3. Click any characteristic to read its value, write to it, or subscribe to notifications.

Web Bluetooth is only available in Chrome, Edge, and Opera on desktop and Android. Connections are always user-initiated and require HTTPS or localhost.
